## 🌍 Who We Are

**ChildrenWhoCode** is a technology-driven initiative passionate about empowering the next generation through technology, creativity, innovation and future-ready skills.

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to explore technology, develop curiosity, experiment with ideas and become a creator of tomorrow.

Our vision goes beyond teaching technology. We want to make learning an engaging journey where young minds discover possibilities, solve problems and build things that matter.

We bring together education, technology, interactive experiences and artificial intelligence to create meaningful digital experiences for the next generation.

## 🚀 What We Do

Our work focuses on building technology-driven educational experiences and tools.

- 💻 **Technology Education** — Making technology and computational thinking accessible to young learners.
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence** — Exploring AI-powered learning experiences and intelligent educational tools.
- 🎮 **Interactive Learning** — Bringing learning to life through games, challenges and hands-on activities.
- 🧠 **Future Skills** — Encouraging creativity, critical thinking, logical reasoning and problem-solving.
- 🌐 **Digital Innovation** — Building platforms and experiences that make education more engaging.
- 🏆 **Student Empowerment** — Helping young learners turn curiosity into creativity and ideas into reality.
